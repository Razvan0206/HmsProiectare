# Niche: architecture and design studios (architecture-studio)

Status: **draft, used on HMS Proiectare (Arad)**. Claims marked **[unverified]** until a second project confirms them.

## Who and why they visit

Private owners planning a house or apartment fit-out, small developers, companies needing offices, halls or farms, public bodies. Visitors ask: have they built what I need, can they take it from idea to permit to site, who do I call. Desktop at work, phone when forwarded a link **[unverified]**.

## Primary conversion (one) and secondary actions

Primary: **start a conversation about a project** (call, e-mail, WhatsApp). Secondary: browse projects by type, see certifications, see services and deliverables (DTAC, PT, SF...).

## Section order (page)

1. Hero: one strong project image, firm name, discipline line, call + e-mail.
2. Services: what they do end to end (consultancy, design, permits, site supervision, project management).
3. Projects: filterable by type (houses, interiors, commercial and industrial, public); detail page with gallery.
4. Process: concept, documentation, permits, execution, supervision (only what the client confirms).
5. Credibility: certifications (ISO), insurance, counts the old site or client can prove.
6. Contact: address, phone, e-mail, map link, hours (placeholder until given).

## Collect from the client

Legal name, CUI, Reg. Com.; architect registration (OAR) and who may sign; team names and roles; best 10-20 projects with photos or renders and permission; project facts (area, year, status); certificates (PDF); insurance; hours; WhatsApp; testimonials with written approval.

## Visual direction (derive, do not copy)

Technical and calm: drawing-sheet logic (hairlines, dimension ticks, small mono labels), sharp corners, one accent from the logo, generous space around project images. Motion low: image reveals, no parallax gimmicks. Imagery leads; type stays quiet.

## Imagery and media

Renders and photos are the content. Keep original aspect ratios in galleries. Old watermarks inside images stay (client's own). Technical drawings can show process, never as the main portfolio visual. Convert to WebP, max 1600 px wide.

## Copy and tone (Romanian)

Concrete deliverables over adjectives. Name document types (DTAC, PT, SF, PUZ) with a short plain-language gloss. No "viziune", "excelență", "soluții inovatoare". Tone formal unless the client says otherwise **[unverified]**.

## SEO and schema

`ProfessionalService` (or `HomeAndConstructionBusiness` if they build). Phrases: "proiectare case Arad", "arhitect Arad", "design interior Arad", "proiectare hale industriale". Project pages get their own title and description.

## Compliance and legal

Footer: legal name, CUI, Reg. Com., registered address (see `10-romania-legal-local.md`). Show certification claims only with the certificate on file. Do not name private clients; show company names only if the client published them already.

## Features worth adding / not worth adding

Worth: filterable portfolio, project detail pages, `tel:`/mailto/WhatsApp. Later: real quote form with endpoint, blog via MDX. Not: auto-rotating sliders as the only navigation, fake counters ("0 proiecte"), generic news copied from other sites.

## Niche anti-patterns

Hero slider that hides the portfolio; stock skyline photos; unlabeled renders presented as built work; empty counters; news feed with third-party content.

## Definition of done

Every project card opens a gallery; filters work without JS libraries; certifications match files; footer legal block complete or visibly placeholder; audit budgets met.
